MutualAid Detroit
Cross-agency resource visibility · Venture 313 Buildathon

When an emergency crosses agency lines, nobody can see what the other agencies have.

Five agencies can share a building and still not share a view of their resources. During a flood or a multi-alarm fire, communication is the first thing to fail — and the question nobody can answer quickly is the simplest one: who has what, and where is it now?

What this is

A shared registry of response resources — vehicles, generators, pumps, crews, shelter capacity — posted by the agencies that own them, placed on a map, and exposed to an AI assistant so dispatch can ask for what they need in plain language instead of working a phone tree.
